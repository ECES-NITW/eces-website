# ECE Society visual system

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

The redesign uses a full-viewport circuit-board field over deep ink blue, white and ice-blue typography, and a luminous procedural 3D signal sculpture. The supplied eagle is rendered directly and crisply in the navigation and footer. Large, condensed display type gives the content its shape; thin rules organize subject and event rows without card grids.

The circuit field stays behind all content: faint PCB traces with pads and vias, signal pulses that travel along them and flash at the vias, and a soft spotlight that brightens the board around the pointer. On fine-pointer devices a custom signal cursor replaces the arrow: a dot pinned to the pointer, a ring that follows with a smooth glowing trail and opens into a probe reticle over links and buttons, a ping on click, and a slight magnetic pull on the main call-to-action buttons. Text fields keep the native cursor. The 3D sculpture occupies the hero background and responds gently to pointer movement. Reduced-motion users receive static versions of these effects, and touch users keep the normal pointer behavior.

The homepage moves from a clear invitation to events through the society's areas of interest and community work. The event page keeps the same world, then places event choices beside a straightforward registration form with clear loading, error, and saved states.

Use the [Electronics Club, IIT Kanpur website](https://eclubiitk.in/) as a reference for organizing content and designing interactions. The Team page adopts its role-based roster, section navigation, and staggered portrait reveals, using the ECE Society palette, typography, and portraits.
